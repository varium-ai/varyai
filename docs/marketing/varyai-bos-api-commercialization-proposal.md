# VaryAI and bos-api: commercialization proposal

> Discussion draft v0.1 · 2026-10-23 · For product and business decision-makers.
>
> The personal/local and self-hosted positioning and hosted-security privacy defaults are approved directions. Packages, prices, quotas, launch dates and service commitments are **not** approved. This is neither a release inventory nor a technical implementation specification or sales promise.

## Executive decision: independent free products, optional ongoing services

**VaryAI should remain a personal local AI gateway; bos-api should be an easy-to-deploy open-source AI gateway and operations tool for individuals and small teams.** Each must be useful alone. Paid value is optional hosted security and reduced maintenance, not a compulsory enterprise suite. bos-agent is a separate exploration, not a dependency.

| Product   | Free foundation / intended user                                                                                                                     | Candidate paid value                                                                                       | Delivery status                                                                         |
| --------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| VaryAI    | Local routing, model configuration and basic protection for individuals using familiar AI clients; no safety model required for baseline protection | Company-operated security review and continuing evaluation/maintenance instead of self-managing a reviewer | Local DLP/tool-observation paths exist; hosted review is a new design and delivery task |
| bos-api   | Proposed GitHub-distributed, usable self-hosted gateway with basic security and operations for developers and small teams                           | Optional advanced security, recurring operations/time-saving services                                      | Repository, deployment and feature completion not supplied for this review              |
| bos-agent | Independently explored Agent product                                                                                                                | Investigate token/credit sales, with Tencent WorkBuddy as a strategic reference only                       | Repository, upstream identity, billing and revenue unverified                           |

The proposition is **less model selection, rule tuning, anomaly monitoring and repetitive work**, not a guarantee against leaks, unsafe execution or financial loss. Free users should receive a complete useful baseline and security fixes, not artificially degraded protection.

### Decision and evidence ledger

- **Approved direction:** VaryAI personal/local first; free baseline and optional self-configured review; optional paid managed security; bos-api open-source/self-hosted for individuals and small teams; each product usable independently. Hosted review requires explicit opt-in, local preprocessing and only necessary snippets; no whole conversation by default or identified raw credentials sent for review.
- **Proposal awaiting approval:** Package names/combinations, monthly allowance plus voluntary top-ups, retaining self-configured options after purchase, pilot order, operations scope, renewal and cancellation experience.
- **Repository evidence, not release acceptance:** VaryAI [DLP architecture](../architecture/security-audit.md), [request inspection](../../src-tauri/src/proxy/security/mod.rs), [tool observations](../architecture/tool-risk.md) and [warning-only types](../../src-tauri/src/services/tool_risk/types.rs). They do not establish end-to-end coverage or a hosted reviewer.
- **Unverified delivery:** Managed service, data transfer and consent design, metering/billing, cross-product integration, bos-api implementation and the supported range of user-provided model review.
- **Business hypotheses:** Willingness to pay, incremental review quality, affordable support, sustainable unit economics and cross-product reuse all need pilots.

The earlier [aaPanel research](../research/aapanel-ai-products-business-model-research.md) considers enterprise and three-layer opportunities. **This later approved personal/self-hosted positioning supersedes those exploratory suggestions as the default route**, without claiming those markets are impossible.

## Users and the reason to pay

For VaryAI, prioritize independent developers and small product owners who may accidentally send deployment credentials, confidential professionals handling client materials, and technically capable holders of valuable personal secrets. Qualify by frequent AI use, meaningful information risk, maintenance burden, comfort with _limited_ cloud review and real willingness to pay—not wealth. Do not claim every credential format is detected, compliance is automatic, or assets are insured. People who cannot permit any external review should be able to stay on the local baseline or an appropriate self-managed local option.

For bos-api, test independent developers needing simpler multi-provider access and small-team operators repeatedly managing member access, limits, channel failures, costs and alerts. Running a public API business is a distinct later segment requiring supplier authorization, accounting and service-liability checks. Personal-subscription forwarding or token resale is **not** its assumed business model.

VaryAI onboarding should first clarify which client traffic is routed and which controls are on. Separate local protection, self-configured review and managed review in the UI. Explain recipient, transferred data, quota and price _before_ service consent: buying is not consent. Display unavailable/unchecked states and remaining allowance, allow cloud review to be switched off, keep cancellation accessible, and do not use scare prompts. Do not prioritize compulsory installation, organization charts, non-bypassable employee policy or enterprise approval flows.

bos-api should borrow aaPanel's low-friction deployment and visual maintenance, not copy its per-IP licensing or price list. Its free version must not lose essential keys, permissions or basic accounts. A potential sub2api-like shape does not establish code reuse or model-supplier rights. If code is reused, check the _specific_ sub2api version and LGPL-3.0 obligations, plus its separate upstream-service notices. This repository's [MIT license](../../LICENSE) says nothing about bos-api's license.

## Shared managed security: consent and narrow scope

The two products could share evaluation, rule maintenance, operations and metering while retaining separate integration and purchases. Neither must install or buy the other; a shared backend does not imply unified accounts or pooled customer data. Hosting operator, infrastructure processors, processing region and access permissions need disclosure before sale; do not silently fall back to an undisclosed third-party model.

**Approved privacy defaults (target design, not implemented hosted functionality):** explicit opt-in; preprocess in the user-controlled environment; send only necessary snippets and minimal context; no entire session by default; never send identified raw private keys or tokens for cloud review. For bos-api, “local” means the customer's self-hosted gateway environment, not necessarily an employee endpoint.

Proposed flow: (1) apply enabled local rules and keep deterministic local blocks in force; (2) mask/remove recognized secrets and extract only candidates worth reviewing; (3) transmit only with appropriate consent to the disclosed service; (4) return a bounded assessment, explanation or **uncertain/unchecked** state, never arbitrary tool authority or automatic rule changes; (5) stop new reviews after consent withdrawal and define in-flight handling and deletion before launch. Incomplete input cannot be silently fixed by uploading full logs, history or files. Masking is imperfect; even minimal snippets can reveal sensitive business information and less context can degrade accuracy. Measure both trade-offs.

A model's low-risk verdict must not override a deterministic local rule. Unknown tool identity or missing context cannot be declared safe. [Tool observations](../architecture/tool-risk.md) are asynchronous warnings/records, **not** proof a tool ran or an execution gate. Do not sell “cloud approval before tool execution,” full prompt-injection prevention or guaranteed leak prevention.

Before a paid launch, specify data categories, purpose, recipients, regions, access, retention/deletion and withdrawal; propose no training on review content by default, with separate permission for any extra use. Minimize content in errors, traces, support and billing independently. Test prompt injection, false decisions, malformed responses, regression and explicit unknown states. Provide understandable coverage and service status rather than a misleading green “safe” indicator.

## Charging, lifecycle and economics

**Candidates, not prices:** pilot a monthly service fee with a defined review allowance and _voluntary_ top-ups; also test prepaid usage for irregular users. bos-api operations can be purchased separately if a repeatable task demonstrably saves time. Avoid unlimited/lifetime managed cloud service before measuring inference and support costs. No price, allowance, seat/device/instance count or launch date is set. Keep three bills distinct: free base software, optional security/operations services, and the user's upstream task-model or self-configured reviewer inference. bos-agent credits are not interchangeable by default.

One review is not one message: a client may generate separate chat/title/retry calls (see [WorkBuddy setup](../guides/workbuddy.md) for routing context, **not** pricing evidence). Define eligible requests, size limits, retries, incomplete assessments, what counts as an attempt/completion/billable unit and auditable invoices. Local rules, audit-only findings and blocks must not be billed as cloud inference. No automatic overage purchase; show warnings without implying that review was completed.

| Event                           | Proposed floor, not an existing service guarantee                                      | Still to decide                                                             |
| ------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Expiry or cancellation          | Preserve configuration and free local baseline; clearly mark end of paid service       | Remaining balance, refunds, renewal rules                                   |
| Allowance exhausted             | Show managed review unavailable, without silent charges or pretending a check occurred | Notifications, top-up and recovery timing                                   |
| Timeout, outage, invalid output | Show unchecked/uncertain; preserve existing local deterministic policy                 | Forward vs block for each supported scenario, timeouts, retries and charges |
| Consent withdrawn               | Stop new cloud review, preserve local baseline                                         | In-flight requests, deletion and accounting retention                       |

Failures in auxiliary logging/usage/observation must not break normal forwarding. Whether a **future synchronous** cloud decision should fail open or closed is not decided and requires distinct technical/security approval and advance user disclosure; current tool warnings must not be repackaged as cloud execution approval. Set auto-renewal, refund, invoice and SLA terms before trial sales; this draft promises no response time, availability or compensation.

**Unit-economics frame:** period service contribution = collected service revenue − inference − hosting/network/storage − payment/refunds − attributable support/security-operations cost. It must additionally support fixed engineering, evaluation, free-rule maintenance, community and acquisition costs; positive contribution is not company profitability. Measure long inputs, heavy users, retries, false positives and human support hours. No revenue, margin or payback forecast without data.

## Verification, rollout and next decisions

The current [DLP design](../architecture/security-audit.md) covers selected outbound request text; default credential/PII actions, persistence normalization and safety failures have specific limits, not universal blocking. [Tool-risk documentation](../architecture/tool-risk.md) says the current worker uses static rules and **no local-model reviewer is connected**. A hosted reviewer therefore needs its own transport, consent and privacy architecture; it cannot be enabled by replacing a local model address with a cloud URL. Existing local-only concepts, if adopted later, need their own review, not a silent override by this proposal.

Pilot VaryAI via client setup and synthetic-data demonstrations, and bos-api via GitHub deployment guidance and repeatable operator tasks—subject to bos-api inventory first. Trial use has the same consent requirements as paid use. Measure first successful call, retention, explicit purchase and renewal, cancellation reasons, support time, review completion/latency, incremental misses/false positives/uncertainty against local rules, and adversarial performance. Use synthetic or explicitly authorized data, never collected real private keys; a high count of blocks does not prove low miss rates.

Suggested **business sequence, not an engineering schedule**:

1. Inventory demonstrable free capabilities, especially bos-api's repository/deployment, and interview target users about actual burden, privacy constraints and willingness to pay.
2. Pilot narrow, consented managed review: compare safety lift, privacy, latency and costs to the local baseline and demonstrate clear failure states and exit.
3. Approve and test limited paid packages and one or two bos-api services, with reconcilable metering, cancellation/refunds and measured costs.
4. Expand only if coverage, reliability, support load and economics remain acceptable. Product, security/engineering and operations owners must approve quantitative gates before each pilot—not treat absent thresholds as passed.

**Separate bos-agent track:** explore a WorkBuddy-inspired token/credit hypothesis only after verifying the actual product/upstream, task costs, supplier terms, failed/retried work and refunds. This is not a claim about WorkBuddy's official pricing or bos-agent's present revenue, and it does not supply either gateway's security controls.

Next approvals: select first review scenarios and bos-api tasks; decide retained self-configured options and packaging; define billing unit, consent/retention, outage/allowance behavior, cancellations and support scope; assign pilot owners, budgets and stop conditions. **Do not launch until the free baseline, coverage, privacy/withdrawal, effects, costs, licensing and supplier authorizations are tested and the relevant terms approved.**
