# Provider Icon Sources

The `provider-*.svg` vendor logos are copied unchanged from the routing
reference's `internal/gui/assets/icons/` directory. Their original names are
`groq.svg`, `together-color.svg`, `fireworks-color.svg`, `ai302-color.svg`, and
`zai.svg`.

The `import-*` files use the same source directory. Their original filenames,
upstream repository, and pinned revision are recorded in
[the third-party source notice](../../../third_party/routing-reference/NOTICE.md).
The full MIT notice is retained in
[the license](../../../third_party/routing-reference/LICENSE).

These logos identify their respective providers; their use does not imply
affiliation or endorsement. Keep the original artwork when renaming assets.
